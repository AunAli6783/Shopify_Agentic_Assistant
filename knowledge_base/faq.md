# Store Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What payment methods are accepted?
- We accept all major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard), Bank Transfers, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

## 2. Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
- Orders can be modified or cancelled within **2 hours** of placement. After 2 hours, orders enter automated warehouse fulfillment and cannot be modified until delivered.

## 3. How do I know if an item is in stock?
- Product availability is displayed in real-time on each product page. If an item is out of stock, you can sign up for restock notifications or ask our AI assistant for similar in-stock alternatives.

## 4. Do you offer warranty on electronics and gear?
- All hardware and electronic items carry an official **1-year manufacturer warranty**. Wear-and-tear and cosmetic damages are excluded from warranty coverage.

## 5. How can I contact customer support?
- Live AI chat is available 24/7 on our storefront. For escalated human support, our staff is available Monday-Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM via email at `support@agentic-ai.com` or WhatsApp at `+92-300-1234567`.
