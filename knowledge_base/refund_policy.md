# Store Return & Refund Policy

## 1. Return Window
- We offer a **30-day return policy**. You have 30 calendar days from the date of delivery to request a return or exchange.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements
To be eligible for a full refund or exchange:
- The item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition that you received it.
- The item must be in its original packaging with all tags, labels, and protective wrapping intact.
- A proof of purchase or order confirmation number is required.

## 3. Non-Returnable Items
The following categories cannot be returned:
- Gift cards and downloadable digital products.
- Personalized, customized, or made-to-order items.
- Perishable goods, personal hygiene, and intimate apparel.
- Final clearance items marked "As-Is" or "Non-Refundable".

## 4. Refund Process
1. Initiate a return by contacting our AI support assistant or emailing `support@agentic-ai.com` with your order number.
2. Once your return is received and inspected at our warehouse, we will send you an email notification.
3. If approved, your refund will be processed to the original payment method within **5-7 business days**.
4. Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds will be issued via bank transfer or store credit voucher.

## 5. Return Shipping Costs
- If the return is due to our error (defective, damaged, or incorrect item received), we will cover the return shipping fee.
- For customer preference returns (wrong size ordered, change of mind), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
