# Store Shipping Policy

## 1. Processing Times
- Orders are processed and dispatched within **1-2 business days** (Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays).
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays will begin processing on the following business day.

## 2. Domestic Shipping Rates & Estimates
- **Standard Shipping (3-5 business days)**: Free on all orders over 5,000 PKR. For orders under 5,000 PKR, a flat rate of 250 PKR applies.
- **Express Shipping (1-2 business days)**: Flat rate of 500 PKR available across major metropolitan cities.

## 3. International Shipping
- We currently ship to selected international destinations including UAE, UK, USA, and Canada.
- Delivery times typically range between **7-14 business days**.
- International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on package weight and destination.
- Customs duties, taxes, and import fees are the responsibility of the recipient.

## 4. Order Tracking
- Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email confirmation containing your courier tracking number.
- Tracking numbers become active within 24 hours of dispatch.

## 5. Damaged or Lost Packages
- If your order arrives damaged, please take photos of the packaging and product and contact customer service within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Lost shipments will be investigated with the courier and replaced or refunded once confirmed.
